FAQ

Answers to your questions.

What is ARCFORM?

ARCFORM is a luxury streetwear brand specializing in precision-crafted garments. Each piece is engineered with architectural precision and produced in limited quantities.

Where is ARCFORM based?

We operate studios in Los Angeles and Tokyo, collaborating internationally with craftspeople and innovators.

How do I determine my size?

We provide detailed size guides on our website. Our garments feature precision tailoring, so fit may differ from standard retail.

What is your return policy?

14-day returns from delivery date for unworn items with original tags. Return shipping is complimentary for defective items.

How long does shipping take?

Standard domestic shipping takes 3-5 business days. International shipping typically arrives within 7-10 business days.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. International costs vary by destination. Customers may be responsible for customs duties.

How should I care for my items?

Care instructions are included with each piece. Most items are best maintained through dry cleaning or spot cleaning.

Are your products sustainable?

Sustainability is core to our mission. We use eco-consciously sourced materials and maintain ethical production practices.

Do you offer discounts?

We do not offer seasonal discounts to protect the integrity and value of our brand.

How can I contact support?

Email hello@arcform.studio or use our contact form. We respond within 24-48 hours.
